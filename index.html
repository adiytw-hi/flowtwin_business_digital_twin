Model assumptions. One SKU, integer stock and quantities, 14 whole days (0–13), FIFO customer order allocation, one supplier PO outstanding at a time, fixed procurement lead time, and same-day receipts before shipments. Carrier capacity is a daily maximum, while a slowdown event applies only on its scheduled day. Unshipped orders remain open. “Backorder” is unshipped demand in excess of currently available stock; "ready queue" is unshipped demand covered by current stock, not hard-reserved. Procurement happens at day's end when inventory position (stock + inbound − outstanding demand) meets the reorder threshold. The fulfillment SLA measures units shipped no later than order day plus the target. Auto PO receipts may occur after the 14-day window. No revenue, payments, costs, supplier capacity, taxes, inter-warehouse moves or real API calls are modeled. Exported values are hypothetical, not operational predictions.
